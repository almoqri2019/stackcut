Frequently asked questions

Do I need to connect my bank to use StackCut?

No. The quick calculator works with three values you enter yourself. The full app supports manual subscription entry and supported CSV imports.

Does StackCut automatically cancel subscriptions?

No. StackCut helps you review costs and possible overlaps. You decide whether to cancel a service and complete the cancellation with its provider.

Does a possible overlap mean I can save that full amount?

No. Similar names or categories can indicate something worth reviewing, but they do not prove two services are interchangeable. Actual savings depend on what you choose to stop paying for.

Is StackCut a replacement for a personal finance app?

Not for everyone. StackCut focuses on software subscription reviews. If you want comprehensive budgeting, credit features, and account-linked transaction tracking, a broader personal finance app may suit you better.
