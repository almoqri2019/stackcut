Important limitations

This estimates indirect consumption taxes on software subscriptions. It is not an income-tax calculator, tax-return tool, tax advice, or a guarantee of a vendor's invoice. Tax applicability may depend on customer type, exact location, product classification, exemptions, tax-inclusive pricing, and supplier obligations.

Automated rates use the EU standard VAT reference from VATcomply, which documents data from the European Commission TEDB. A standard rate may not apply to a particular purchase. Retrieval time is not a legal effective date. Outside this coverage, no rate is assumed; a manual rate is user-supplied.

Tax rounds to the currency's minor-unit precision per period. Monthly equivalents use 52 weeks and 12 months per year. No currency conversion is performed.
