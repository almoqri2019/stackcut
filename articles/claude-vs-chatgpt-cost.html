Claude Pro vs ChatGPT Plus: A Real Monthly Cost Breakdown

Compare the work each plan does for you and its normalized price. If one plan bills weekly, $20/week is about $86.67 per month—not $80.

Compare plans fairly

Plan names and feature lists change. Start with the current price, renewal period, and included features shown in your own account. Prices can vary by location, tax, promotions, and purchase route, so treat any old comparison price as a snapshot rather than a quote.

Then write down the tasks you actually use each assistant for. Examples might include drafting, coding help, research, document review, or brainstorming. The best fit is the plan that handles your important work well at a cost you are comfortable renewing.

Avoid assuming that two similarly named plans have identical limits or features. Check the current plan details directly before making a decision.

Normalize the cost before comparing

Convert each price to a monthly equivalent. For weekly billing, multiply by 52 and divide by 12. For yearly billing, divide by 12. Monthly billing stays as stated. Keep taxes or one-time charges separate when they do not recur.

Example: $20 billed weekly

$20 × 52 ÷ 12 = $86.67 per average month. Multiplying by four gives $80, which understates the full-year average.

Example: $200 billed yearly

$200 ÷ 12 = $16.67 per month. The account may still charge the full $200 at renewal, so track the renewal date as well as the monthly equivalent.

Compare the workflow, not just the labels

Pick a few representative tasks and try them in both products. Note the output quality, time saved, and any plan limits you reach. Your own workflow is more informative than a generic ranking.

Use a short, repeatable test

  1. Choose two or three tasks you do regularly.
  2. Try the same task in both tools.
  3. Record which output you would actually use and how much editing it needed.
  4. Review the current plan terms and normalized monthly cost.

When keeping both subscriptions makes sense

Keeping both may be reasonable if they serve different recurring needs, if both save enough time to justify their cost, or if a specific workflow genuinely depends on each. If their roles overlap, try using one as your primary plan for a full billing period before renewing both.

Separate cost from usage

Billing cycle answers how the vendor charges. Usage frequency answers how often you use the service. Track each separately; a weekly user might have monthly billing, and a yearly plan can still go unused.

FAQ

Which costs more, Claude Pro or ChatGPT Plus?

Prices and plan terms can change. Check both accounts and normalize their billing cycles to compare current monthly costs.

How much is $20 a week per month?

$20 × 52 ÷ 12 = $86.67 per average month, rounded to cents.

Can Claude Pro replace ChatGPT Plus?

It depends on your tasks and preferred workflows. Test the work you actually do before canceling either plan.

Should I compare features or price first?

Define your needs, check current plan details, and compare normalized costs against how often you use each tool.

How do I decide whether to keep both?

Review whether both perform distinct valuable work and whether each monthly equivalent fits your budget.
