How to Cancel ChatGPT Plus: Step-by-Step + Email Template

Cancel through the same billing route you used to subscribe, then verify the next renewal is stopped. Use this checklist to keep the process clear.

  3. Cancel through an app store

Before you cancel

First identify the account and payment route tied to ChatGPT Plus. If you have multiple email addresses, check each account’s plan settings and search your receipts for the subscription provider.

  • Save any information you need from the account.
  • Note the renewal date and billing provider.
  • Use the account’s official settings or app-store subscription page; never share your password with someone offering to cancel for you.
The menu labels can change. Look for plan, subscription, or billing settings and follow the current on-screen cancellation flow.

Cancel a subscription started on the web

  1. Sign in to the ChatGPT account that has the paid plan.
  2. Open your profile menu and go to settings.
  3. Open the plan or subscription area and choose its manage or cancel option.
  4. Review the confirmation screen and submit the cancellation request.
  5. Return to the plan page and check that it shows an end date or that renewal is canceled.

If you cannot find the subscription, confirm you are signed into the correct account and check the receipt for the billing provider.

Cancel through Apple or Google

If you started the subscription in a mobile app, billing may be managed by the app store rather than the web account.

Apple subscriptions

On your Apple device, open your Apple Account subscription settings, find the ChatGPT subscription, and follow the cancellation steps. Confirm its status in the same subscription list.

Google Play subscriptions

Open Google Play’s payments and subscriptions area using the Google account that made the purchase. Select the subscription and cancel renewal there.

Cancel with the provider shown on the receipt. Removing an app from your device does not itself stop a subscription.

Confirm the cancellation and keep a record

Save the confirmation screen or email, and note the date access is scheduled to end. Check the account again before the next billing date if the status is unclear.

If you are still charged

Compare the charge date and merchant with your confirmation. Contact the billing provider listed on your receipt and provide the account email, transaction date, and cancellation confirmation. Never include a password or full card number.

Do not confuse cancellation with deletion

Deleting an account and stopping a recurring payment are separate actions. Verify that the subscription’s renewal status changed before taking any optional account-deletion step.

A concise cancellation email template

Subject: Please cancel my ChatGPT Plus subscription

Hello,

Please cancel renewal of the ChatGPT Plus subscription associated with [account email]. Please confirm in writing the date access ends and that no further renewal is scheduled.

Thank you,
[Name]

Send a message only through an official support channel. The email template does not replace the in-account or app-store cancellation flow when one is available.

FAQ

How do I cancel ChatGPT Plus on the web?

Sign in to the subscribed account, open its settings and plan or billing controls, complete the cancellation prompts, and verify the renewal status.

What if I subscribed through Apple or Google?

Cancel in the Apple or Google account used for the purchase; that store manages the subscription renewal.

Will I lose access as soon as I cancel?

Access may continue through the paid period. Check the end date in the subscription settings.

Should I delete my account to stop billing?

No. Treat billing cancellation and account deletion as separate actions and verify renewal is canceled.

What should I include in a cancellation email?

Include the account email and plan name, request stopped renewal, and ask for written confirmation. Do not send passwords or full payment details.
